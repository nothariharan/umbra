When Builder issues a new SUBMIT rev after S3-U9 branch realignment, that SUBMIT supersedes all prior SUBMIT pins for S3-U8 unified ACCEPT. Verifier, breaker, and uireviewer ACCEPT at rev R counts toward certification only if R equals the latest Builder SUBMIT rev on record at certifier gate time. ACCEPT on an earlier SUBMIT pin (e.g. orphaned 1ad60682143c after SUBMIT 55207f5fd143) is historical and must not be combined with ACCEPT on a different rev.
